Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Faceless Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Little Quiet Things YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google sign-in, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read basic statistics (views, likes, comments) of those videos.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token and statistics stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Faceless Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.
